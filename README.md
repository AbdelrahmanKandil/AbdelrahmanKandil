## 🚀 About Me
I'm a Senior Automation Test Engineer at VOIS, where I test TOBi, Vodafone's AI assistant. I work on LLM evaluation, conversational AI quality, and UI and API automation with Playwright. Before VOIS, I worked at Sumerge and KLAO, delivering manual and automated testing across Agile projects.

My background in embedded systems gives me a technical depth in software quality. I trained at the Information Technology Institute (ITI) and the National Telecommunication Institute (NTI), where I built foundations in C/C++, RTOS, Python, and embedded development. At Valeo Testing Academy, I deepened my knowledge of software testing life cycles, black-box and white-box techniques, and test case design.

I'm passionate about software quality, problem-solving, and continuous learning, especially where AI systems and reliability meet.
